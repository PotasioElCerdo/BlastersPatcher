Patch mods into your game

This site patches a mod's files directly into your game and its update, then gives you the patched files back. It was made for BLASTERS++, but it also works with other mods and other Yo-kai Watch games.

Patching only one of them is possible, but it's recommended to patch both the game and the update.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Files must be decrypted dumps, and big ones need a few GB of free memory.
